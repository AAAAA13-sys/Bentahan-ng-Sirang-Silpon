# Frontend Design

Approach this as the design lead at a design studio known for giving every client a distinct visual identity that is not mistaken for anyone else's. Avoid cliché, templated AI aesthetics. Make deliberate, opinionated choices about palette, typography, and layout that are specific to this brief.

## Core Anti-AI Guidelines

Avoid these common AI-generated design tells:
1. **Generic radial gradient blobs**: Stop putting large, blurry purple/indigo gradient circles in the top corners of dark dashboards.
2. **Cliché gradient headlines**: Avoid "bg-gradient-to-r from-white via-slate-200 to-indigo-200 bg-clip-text". Use crisp, intentional, solid typographic hierarchy.
3. **Gratuitous pill badges & all-caps labels**: Stop plastering "UPPERCASE TRACKING-WIDER" everywhere. Use purposeful micro-typography.
4. **Hero marketing fluff on working dashboards**: Power users and developers do not want a 300px marketing banner inside their active work surface. Put the workspace front and center.
5. **Over-decorating with icons**: Icons must serve a spatial navigation purpose, not act as visual noise.
6. **Low density**: Give engineering tools high data density, clean tabular and kanban layouts, crisp 1px borders, and clear status tokens.
